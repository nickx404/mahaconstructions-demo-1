The user settled on this order:

1. Hero/introduction: typographic, asymmetric, and image-free. Use “ABOUT MAHA CONSTRUCTIONS” and “BUILDING WITH PURPOSE. GROWING WITH TRUST.” over restrained CSS architectural linework and a low-opacity “M” or “MC”. 8. Footer.

Make About feel editorial and story-driven, with large photography, typography, timeline treatment, and whitespace. Avoid repeating the homepage copy wholesale.
